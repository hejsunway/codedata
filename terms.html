2 Daily Reflection Requirement

  • Learners are required to submit daily reflections documenting what they have learned during each active study session.
  • A minimum of 90 reflection submissions per year (equivalent to 90 days of active learning per year) must be completed to maintain access to certifications, mentorship, and career support features.
  • Each reflection must be a genuine, personal account of the learner's own learning experience for that day. Reflections must be submitted through the official ITAI learner portal.
