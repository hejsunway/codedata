Common Questions

Everything you need to know about the program

Do I need prior experience with data or coding?

No! We start from absolute basics. Our program is designed for complete beginners. We progressively build up your skills from foundational concepts to advanced techniques.

What's the time commitment required?

Typically 10–15 hours per week for 4–6 months. You can customize your pace based on your schedule. We offer flexible learning with recorded sessions and live office hours.

Will I get a certification?

Yes! You'll receive a Professional Certificate upon completion, recognized by leading tech companies. You'll also build a portfolio of real projects to showcase to employers.

What's the money-back guarantee?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with the first module, we'll refund your full payment with no questions asked.

Will this help me get a job?

Absolutely! 87% of our graduates land data analytics roles within 6 months. We provide career support, resume review, interview prep, and job placement assistance throughout the program.
